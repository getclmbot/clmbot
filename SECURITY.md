# Security Policy

Do not publish unresolved security vulnerabilities in public Issues, Discussions, comments, or social media.

Send reports to `security@clmbot.org` and include:

- the affected clmbot version;
- sanitized reproduction steps;
- the potential impact; and
- your preferred contact method.

Do not send private keys, access credentials, complete certificates, or customer data that is not required to reproduce the issue. If the repository Security page offers **Report a vulnerability**, you may use that option to start a private report.

SslTrus Inc. will acknowledge and coordinate reports on a best-effort basis. No fixed response or disclosure timeline is guaranteed.
